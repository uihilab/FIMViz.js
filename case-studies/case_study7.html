Case Study 7 — Rain on the Amite, and the reach that carried it

A 1 km National Water Model forcing grid animated, windowed, reduced and summarised in the browser against a basin fetched live from the USGS Network Linked Data Index, with the simulated reach response beside it. Protocol: CASE_STUDY_PROTOCOL.md §0 and §2.

The question. Rain fell on the Amite River basin from 10 to 17 August 2016 and the river at Denham Springs reached a record stage. Where in the basin did the rain fall, how much of the storm total landed inside the drainage area rather than beside it, and how did the simulated reach at the basin outlet respond?
