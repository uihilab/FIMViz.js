FIMViz — lazy Dataset reproject

Exercises the lazy-Dataset ADT (docs/DECISIONS_TRADEOFFS_INCOMPLETE_ITEMS.md §1.1): FimViz.parseFile(file) → a Dataset in its native CRS, then ds.reproject(crs) (a lazy op — nothing warps yet) → ds.grid() forces the GDAL warp and decodes the result. The original Dataset is never mutated. Serve from the repo root (npx serve .) and open /examples/reproject.html — ES modules and fetch fail on file://. The first warp downloads GDAL wasm/data (~38 MB) from the CDN.
